Four Foundational Safeguards

Built to protect student rights, ensure safety, and run university fitness facilities with zero attendance disputes.

Zero-Proxy Check-In

Attendance is never recorded via static screenshots, shared barcodes, or proxy roll-calls. Each student's digital card renders an encrypted, rotating token valid strictly for that day's scheduled session.

At the gym turnstile, gym trainers scan the code to inspect the student's official registered photograph and academic year, confirming physical identity before granting entry.

Protected Slot Transfers

Timetable changed? Lab timing clashing with your evening workout? Apply for any available slot across C. V. Raman, Ganga, Krishna, or Narmada without forfeiting your active membership.

Under GymSlot's atomic transaction policy, your current slot remains fully active and usable until sports administration confirms the transfer destination.

Fair FCFS Waitlists

Every registration request is assigned an authoritative UTC timestamp by the server. When high-demand slots reach capacity, students join a deterministic first-come, first-served queue with live position tracking.

Serializable database isolation guarantees that a slot configured for 120 students never admits a 121st student, eliminating overcrowding at squat racks and bench presses.

Absence Policy

Gym slots are high-demand university resources. GymSlot automatically monitors weekly attendance frequency based on the Sports Department's approved absence policy.

Students approaching absence thresholds receive clear in-app alerts and email warnings. Prolonged inactivity reclaims the slot and instantly promotes the next waitlisted applicant.

Can I switch gym slots if my class timetable changes?

Yes. You can use the Change Gym Slot feature in your student dashboard at any time. Your current allocation remains completely active and secure while your transfer request is pending. If approved, the slot swap completes atomically.

How does desk check-in work without physical plastic cards?

Open GymSlot on your mobile phone to show your Digital Gym Card. The card generates an encrypted, short-lived QR code. The trainer scans your screen, confirms your registered student photo on their desk display, and logs your attendance in under a second.

What is the absence policy and how does slot reclamation work?

The Sports Department monitors regular attendance so allocated equipment isn't left idle while peers wait. If you miss consecutive workout weeks without an approved academic or medical leave, the system issues warning notices before releasing the slot to the next waitlisted applicant.

What happens if my phone battery dies or the camera fails to scan?

Authorized gym staff and trainers have an approved manual check-in fallback. They can search by your unique AP Registration Number, verify your official college photo and allocated slot on their terminal, and mark attendance with full audit trail logging.

Who manages facility maintenance and training guidance?

All four facilities are staffed by certified trainers and student sports interns under the direct supervision of the SRM University-AP Sports Department, ensuring equipment safety, spotter assistance, and hygiene.
